Good Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you ship my photos anywhere?

No — everything is handled locally in the DFW area. Drop off at our Flower Mound location inside the GEICO office at 4630 Long Prairie Rd, Ste 200, or David comes to you. Your originals are never shipped away or outsourced.

Drop-off or pickup?

Both. Drop off and pick up at our Flower Mound location, or David will come to you — whichever is easier.

How long does it take?

Most photo projects finish within 1–2 weeks. Tape orders of 1–5 tapes are typically done within a week.

What formats do you accept?

Loose photos, albums, VHS, Hi8, 8mm, Digital8, and MiniDV tapes, plus 35mm and disposable-camera film negatives. Slide scanning is not offered at this time.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes — a $99 project minimum. Photos, albums, and tapes can be combined to reach it. Pay by cash, Zelle, Venmo, or card.
