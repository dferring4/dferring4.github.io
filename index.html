● Good questions

Before you hand over the box.

Do you ship my photos anywhere?

No — everything is handled locally in the DFW area. Drop off at our Flower Mound location inside the GEICO office at 4630 Long Prairie Rd, Ste 200, or David comes to you. Your originals are never shipped away or outsourced.

Drop-off or pickup?

Both. Drop off and pick up at our Flower Mound location, or David will come to you — whichever is easier.

How long does it take?

Most photo projects finish within 1–2 weeks. Tape orders of 1–5 tapes are typically done within a week. Tapes capture in real time — a 2-hour tape takes 2 hours.

What formats — and do you do negatives?

VHS, Hi8, 8mm, Digital8, and MiniDV tapes, digitized locally. Film negatives (35mm and disposable-camera) are quoted individually. Slide scanning is not offered at this time.

Standard vs Enhanced?

Standard is a faithful archival scan. Enhanced adds AI color, contrast, and brightness correction, and you keep both versions. Heavily damaged photos can be hand-restored.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes — a $99 project minimum. Photos, albums, and tapes can be combined to reach it. Pay by cash, Zelle, Venmo, or card.
