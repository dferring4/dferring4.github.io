About Digitize DFW

A local, owner-run business preserving DFW's family memories — one shoebox, tape, and negative at a time.

Meet David

Hi, I'm David Ferring

Digitize DFW is a local, owner-run business right here in Flower Mound, Texas. When you hand over your memories, you're not shipping them to a warehouse or a stranger across the country — you're handing them to a neighbor who handles every step personally: pickup or drop-off, digitizing, restoration, and delivery.

With more than 25 years in insurance and information technology, I understand what trust, security, and attention to detail really mean — especially with one-of-a-kind originals that can't be replaced. Digitize DFW is a licensed Texas LLC, and your photos and tapes never leave the DFW area or get outsourced.

Questions before you start? Call or text me directly. I pick up.
