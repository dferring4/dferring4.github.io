Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before you bring in your photos and tapes. Still have a question? Call or text David anytime.

Do you ship my photos anywhere?

No — everything is handled locally in the DFW area. Drop off at our Flower Mound location inside the GEICO office at 4630 Long Prairie Rd, Ste 200, or David comes to you. Your originals are never shipped away or outsourced.

Do you have a drop-off location, or do you pick up?

Both. Drop off and pick up at our Flower Mound location, or David will come to you — whichever is easier.

What areas do you serve?

Flower Mound, Lewisville, Highland Village, Coppell, Grapevine, Southlake, Denton, Frisco, Plano and most of Denton, Dallas, Tarrant, and Collin counties.

How long does it take?

Most photo projects finish within 1–2 weeks. Tape orders of 1–5 tapes are typically done within a week. Tapes capture in real time, so a 2-hour tape takes 2 hours.

What do I receive at the end?

Your originals back, plus organized digital files on an engraved flash drive in a keepsake tin. A short-term download link is available for smaller projects. No cloud account, app, or subscription.

What photo formats and sizes do you accept?

Loose photos, prints, and albums — including magnetic and sticky-page albums, odd sizes, and cardboard-backed prints. Shoeboxes hold up to about 1,000 photos.

What video and tape formats do you accept?

VHS, Hi8, 8mm, Digital8, and MiniDV camcorder tapes, all digitized locally in real time.

Do you do film negatives?

Yes — 35mm and disposable-camera negatives, single frames or full strips. Negative projects are quoted individually; just text David a photo of what you have.

Do you scan slides?

Slide scanning is not offered at this time. For photos, albums, tapes, film negatives, and restoration, David has you covered.

What's the difference between Standard and Enhanced?

Standard is a faithful, archival-quality scan. Enhanced adds AI color, contrast, and brightness correction, and you keep both versions.

Can you fix damaged or faded photos?

Yes. Photo restoration repairs tears, creases, fading, and water or stain damage by hand, finished with AI enhancement. Restoration starts at $19.99 per photo, with complex work quoted individually.

Is there a minimum order, and how do I pay?

There's a $99 project minimum — photos, albums, and tapes can be combined to reach it. Pay by cash, Zelle, Venmo, or credit card.

Will my photos get mixed with other customers' photos?

Never. Your originals are kept together and handled as a single project from start to finish.

Ready to preserve your memories?

Call or text David for a friendly, no-pressure quote — serving Flower Mound and the DFW metroplex.
