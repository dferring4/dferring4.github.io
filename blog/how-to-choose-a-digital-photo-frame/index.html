How to Choose a Digital Photo Frame (and Load It the Easy Way)

A digital frame is one of the best ways to enjoy digitized photos — and a wonderful gift. Here is how to pick one.

By David Ferring · Digitize DFW · Flower Mound, TX

Once your photos are digitized, a digital frame is one of the most joyful ways to enjoy them every day — and a wonderful gift for parents and grandparents. Here is what David Ferring at Digitize DFW suggests looking for.

Screen size and resolution

A 10-inch screen is a comfortable, versatile size for a shelf or side table. Look for a higher-resolution IPS display so photos look crisp and colors stay true from an angle.

Ease of use matters most

The fanciest frame is useless if it is frustrating to set up. For older family members especially, a frame that simply turns on and plays — no Wi-Fi, no app, no account — is often the best choice.

Loading it is the hard part

Choosing the frame is easy; getting your photos onto it is where people get stuck. That is exactly what Easy Street is for: David hand-selects your best photos, loads them onto a quality 10-inch frame, and places it in your home — frame included, no Wi-Fi or apps required — for $229.

A gift that lands every time

A frame preloaded with decades of family photos is the kind of gift that makes people tear up. If you have a shoebox of photos waiting, this is a perfect reason to finally digitize them. Serving Flower Mound and the DFW metroplex.
